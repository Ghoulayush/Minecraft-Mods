# VISIBILITY MODS

> NOTE
> Visibility mods maximize competitive clarity by reducing screen shake, dampening visual clutter, adjusting lighting levels, and providing precision debug outlines.

> TIP
> Adjusting camera shake via **BetterHurtCam** or minimizing fire height can dramatically improve tracking accuracy during high-intensity engagements.

## 1. BactroMod

- A versatile client-side visual clarity and utility suite designed for competitive play.
- Includes Fullbright gamma adjustments, lower first-person fire rendering, lower held shield positioning, and removal of pumpkin overlay blur.
- Features fog disable toggles and ensures maps remain fully readable while riding boats.

## 2. Combat Hitboxes

- Overhauls Minecraft's default F3+B debug hitbox rendering to provide maximum competitive precision.
- Customize hitbox border line thickness, assign distinct colors to different entity components (eyes, body, reach), and toggle individual bounding boxes on or off.
- Essential for tracking fast-moving opponents, Elytra gliders, and End Crystals.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Combat Hitboxes](https://modrinth.com/mod/combat-hitboxes)

## 3. BetterHurtCam

- A modern replacement for the classic NoHurtCam mod that gives you complete control over damage screen shaking.
- Adjust the intensity of the hurt camera bobbing using a smooth slider, or eliminate it entirely so your aim remains steady when taking damage.
- Can be toggled on or adjusted on the fly with custom keybindings.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [BetterHurtCam](https://modrinth.com/mod/betterhurtcam)

## 4. Sodium Extra

- A feature-rich companion add-on for Sodium that introduces dozens of visual settings and performance toggles.
- Fine-tune particle rendering, disable visual clutter (such as excessive potion swirls or explosion debris), adjust fog start distances, and enable true dark lighting.
- Allows disabling sky elements (sun, moon, stars) to maximize FPS and horizon clarity in PvP arenas.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Sodium Extra](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium-extra)

## 5. Mas Effects

- Tailors and tones down overwhelming visual effects during modern Minecraft combat.
- Allows reducing the opacity and scale of Mace smash shockwaves, reducing blinding totem pop particle clouds, and customizing Ender Pearl trail effects.
- Prevents particle spam from obstructing opponent visibility during fast-paced fights.
