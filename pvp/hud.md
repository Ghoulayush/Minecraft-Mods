# HUD & OVERLAY MODS

> NOTE
> HUD and overlay mods display real-time tactical data such as health accuracy, armor durability, active potion durations, hunger saturation, and equipment status without opening inventory menus.

> TIP
> All HUD mods in this section are client-side and can be repositioned or toggled in-game to maintain a clean, uncluttered screen layout.

## 1. AppleSkin

- Adds comprehensive hunger and saturation indicators directly to your user interface.
- Visualizes potential food restoration on the hunger bar when holding food items.
- Displays saturation and exhaustion levels, along with exact nutrition values in item tooltips.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [AppleSkin](https://modrinth.com/mod/appleskin)

## 2. Crosshair Addons

- Enriches your crosshair with dynamic tactical indicators and cooldown cues.
- Features Elytra flight status indicators, firework rocket duration timers, and projectile hitmarkers.
- Keeps critical combat cues centered near your crosshair so your eyes never have to leave the target.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Crosshair Addons](https://modrinth.com/mod/crosshair-addons-public)

## 3. InventoryHUD+

- Renders essential inventory information directly on your main gameplay screen.
- Displays current inventory slot contents, active potion effects with duration timers, and armor/hand equipment durability.
- Highly customizable layout with toggleable panels and individual positioning controls.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [InventoryHUD+](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventoryhudplus)

## 4. Shield Statuses

- Renders customizable color overlays on players' shields to visually indicate their current blocking state.
- Clearly displays whether an opponent's shield is actively blocking, taking a hit, or disabled and on cooldown.
- Eliminates guesswork in shield-heavy combat engagements.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Shield Statuses](https://modrinth.com/mod/shield-statuses)

## 5. Shulker Box Tooltip

- Allows players to preview the contents of shulker boxes, ender chests, and bundles directly by hovering over them in inventories.
- Eliminates the need to place containers down during high-stakes combat to locate potions, crystals, or gear.
- Pressing Shift reveals a full visual container grid.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip)

## 6. Simple Sprint Display

- Adds a minimalist, lightweight HUD element displaying your current sprinting state.
- Clearly indicates whether sprint is active or toggled, preventing accidental walk engagements during critical chases or escapes.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Simple Sprint Display](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-sprint-display)

## 7. TotemCounter

- Tracks and counts Totem of Undying usage for both yourself and opponents.
- Displays the number of totems an opponent has popped directly above their nametag.
- Displays an on-screen counter showing how many backup totems remain in your inventory.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [TotemCounter](https://modrinth.com/mod/totemcounter)

## 8. True Health

- Fixes Minecraft's misleading vanilla heart rounding by rendering accurate, fractional health points down to precise values.
- Prevents situations where a player appears to have a full heart remaining while actually surviving on a fraction of a health point.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [True Health](https://modrinth.com/mod/true-health)

## 9. uku's Armor HUD

- A clean, vanilla-styled armor widget displaying equipped armor pieces and held items along with their numeric durability.
- Features dynamic color coding (green to red) to warn when armor pieces are near breaking point during extended PvP battles.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [uku's Armor HUD](https://modrinth.com/mod/ukus-armor-hud)

## 10. Tier Tagger

- A competitive PvP utility that integrates official tier rankings directly into Minecraft.
- Displays player tier rankings (from MCTiers and PVPTiers) alongside nametags and in the player tab list.
- Lets you instantly gauge an opponent's skill bracket and rank in competitive combat gamemodes.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Tier Tagger](https://modrinth.com/mod/tiertagger)

## 11. Effect Timer Plus

- Enhances status effect icons by overlaying accurate countdown timers and potency level numerals directly onto each effect icon.
- Lets you know exactly how many seconds remain on your Strength, Speed, Resistance, or Fire Resistance buffs at a glance.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Effect Timer Plus](https://modrinth.com/mod/effecttimerplus)
