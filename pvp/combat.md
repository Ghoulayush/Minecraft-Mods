# COMBAT MODS

> NOTE
> Combat mods refine mechanics, optimize entity interactions, fix desynchronization glitches, and synchronize competitive animations without altering vanilla balance.

> TIP
> Most combat mods are purely client-side and allowed on competitive PvP networks. Always review individual server rules regarding client-side optimization mods like crystal modifiers.

## 1. Marlow's Crystal Optimizer

- A high-performance client-side utility designed specifically for End Crystal PvP (CPvP).
- Fixes the vanilla delay where players must wait for server confirmation before an exploded crystal entity is removed from the screen.
- Removes crystals client-side the moment they are struck, allowing immediate placement and detonation of subsequent crystals even on higher ping.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Marlow's Crystal Optimizer](https://modrinth.com/mod/marlow-crystal-optimizer)

## 2. Hero's Elytra Optimizer (Lytra)

- Resolves long-standing vanilla client-server synchronization bugs surrounding Elytra flight.
- Eliminates the visual glitch and delay where a player continues gliding even after their Elytra has been swapped for a chestplate or broken.
- Ensures immediate, accurate transitions between gliding and grounded combat states.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Hero's Elytra Optimizer](https://modrinth.com/mod/lytra)

## 3. Complete Shield Fixes

- A dedicated fix mod that resolves multiple broken vanilla shield mechanics and visual glitches.
- Fixes the desynchronization bug where a player is actively blocking on their screen, but opponents see an unblocked player model.
- Restores missing shield-hit and axe-disable audio cues, providing critical feedback when an opponent's shield is deactivated.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Complete Shield Fixes](https://modrinth.com/mod/complete-shield-fixes)

## 4. Not Enough Animations

- Brings essential first-person player animations over to the third-person perspective.
- Displays realistic third-person animations when other players are eating, drinking potions, holding maps, drawing bows, or blocking with shields.
- Greatly enhances combat readability by letting you clearly see what opponents are doing in real time.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Not Enough Animations](https://modrinth.com/mod/not-enough-animations)

## 5. Vidalia

- A customization mod that brings back legacy Minecraft combat aesthetics and animations.
- Restores classic 1.7 / 1.8 sword blocking animations, old swing rhythms, and vintage eating poses while running modern Minecraft versions.
- Highly customizable with YetAnotherConfigLib (YACL) for fine-tuned animation preferences.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Vidalia](https://modrinth.com/mod/vidalia)

## 6. CPvP

- A focused Crystal PvP utility and texture suite built to optimize competitive crystal combat.
- Lowers held shields, scales down totem pop overlays, and dampens screen-covering explosion smoke.
- Reduces clutter and maximizes frame rates during intense crystal detonations.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [CPvP](https://modrinth.com/mod/cpvp)
