# ANIMATION MODS

> NOTE
> Animation mods transform how the player model moves and interacts in first-person and third-person perspectives, bringing fluid kinetic motion to Minecraft.

> TIP
> The **Punchy!** ecosystem is modular. You can install **Punchy!** for first-person gameplay animations, **Punchy! Moves** for third-person player models, and companion addons like **Tiny Takeover** for interactive baby animal animations.

## 1. Punchy! (First-Person Animations)

- A flagship first-person animation overhaul mod that brings smooth, dynamic, and expressive arm and item animations to Minecraft.
- Features tailored animations for swinging tools, wielding weapons, drawing bows, eating food, drinking potions, and interacting with blocks.
- Gives first-person combat and mining a punchy, responsive visual weight.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Punchy!](https://modrinth.com/mod/punchy-fpa)

## 2. Punchy! Moves

- The third-person counterpart to *Punchy!*, dedicated to comprehensive player-character movement animations and dynamic camera work.
- Overhauls full-body motion for sprinting, jumping, swimming, climbing ladders, riding mounts, mining, and melee combat.
- Includes a smooth, dynamic third-person camera system that makes third-person play enjoyable and responsive.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Punchy! Moves](https://modrinth.com/mod/punchymoves)

## 3. Punchy! x Tiny Takeover

- An official animation and interaction expansion within the Punchy ecosystem.
- Allows players to pick up, hold, and interact with baby mobs (such as baby wolves, kittens, and small pets) with dedicated first-person and third-person animations.
- Features interactive actions including playing with pets, throwing sticks, and whistling to call them back.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Punchy! x Tiny Takeover](https://modrinth.com/mod/tiny-takeover-x-punchy)
