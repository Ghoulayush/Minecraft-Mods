# SOUND & AMBIENT AUDIO MODS

> NOTE Audio mods add rich environmental layers, realistic acoustics, cave reverberation, and tactile interaction sounds to Minecraft.

> TIP **Sound Physics Remastered** simulates how sound travels through real physical geometries. It can be paired with **AmbientSounds** and **Presence Footsteps** for a studio-quality soundscape.

## 1. AmbientSounds

* The premier ambient soundscape mod that dynamically fills Minecraft's world with living background audio.
* Adds rich, layered sound environments unique to each biome: songbirds chirping and leaves rustling in forests, wind howling across snowy mountain ridges, distant ocean wave swells, and night crickets.
* Deep underground, it introduces unsettling cave echoes, water droplets, and ambient rumble.
* Highly customizable volume and frequency controls for each individual sound layer.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [AmbientSounds](https://modrinth.com/mod/ambientsounds)

## 2. Sound Physics Remastered

* Introduces realistic sound physics, including acoustic attenuation, sound reverberation, and obstacle absorption.
* Sound dynamically echoes through large caverns, deep ravines, mine shafts, and grand cathedrals based on room volume and materials.
* Audio is realistically muffled when passing through solid block walls or around corners.
* Fully optimized and compatible with multiplayer proximity mods like Simple Voice Chat.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Sound Physics Remastered](https://modrinth.com/mod/sound-physics-remastered)

## 3. ExtraSounds Next

* Adds tactile, responsive audio feedback to inventory management and UI interactions.
* Unique click and drop sounds for different item materials (e.g. wood, stone, glass, metal, armor).
* Features hotbar scrolling clicks, creative menu tab sounds, and typing/chat notification audio.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [ExtraSounds Next](https://modrinth.com/mod/extrasounds-next)
