# MODS FOR FOOTSTEPS AND MOVEMENT AUDIO

> NOTE Footstep audio mods dramatically increase immersion by replacing Minecraft's basic, repetitive walking sounds with surface-aware acoustics, physical weight, and armor rattle.

> TIP Most footstep mods are purely client-side. They pair exceptionally well with environmental sound mods like **Sound Physics Remastered** to produce authentic echoes in caves and halls.

## 1. Presence Footsteps

- An advanced, dynamic movement acoustic overhaul that completely replaces vanilla Minecraft's footstep engine.
- Rich, material-aware audio feedback for every surface in the game, distinguishing between dry wood, creaking floorboards, crunchy gravel, wet mud, snow, and resonant metal.
- Differentiates movement dynamics: walking, running, sprinting, crouching, jumping, and hard landing.
- Adds subtle armor rattle sounds while moving and wet squelching footsteps when stepping out of water or walking through rain.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Presence Footsteps](https://modrinth.com/mod/presence-footsteps)

## 2. Auditory

- A quality-of-life audio mod that enriches block-stepping and interaction sounds throughout the game.
- Adds unique, satisfying step and placement acoustics for specific blocks that vanilla otherwise lumps into generic sound categories.
- Covers interaction audio cues for eating, drinking, placing blocks, and swapping tools.

Download : ![Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/favicon.ico) [Auditory](https://modrinth.com/mod/auditory-continued)
