# Introduction

> TIP New to modded Minecraft? Start with the [Launcher setup](launcher-setup.md) page, then head to [Performance](../performance/overview.md) to begin improving your frame rates.

This guide is a curated knowledge base for making Minecraft faster, better-looking, more immersive, and better suited for PvP. It is organized around goals rather than individual projects, so you can jump straight to the area you care about most.

## What you will find here

* **Performance** — mods and configuration to improve frame rates, reduce stutter, and lower memory usage.
* **Atmosphere** — ambient sound, weather, particles, footsteps, and small immersive touches.
* **Visuals** — graphics, animations, lighting, and shader setups.
* **PvP** — combat, HUD, visibility, and competitive-oriented changes.
* **Resource packs** — textures that change how the game looks.
* **Shaders** — lighting and rendering overhauls.
* **Setups** — curated combinations of mods, packs, and shaders for a specific goal.
* **Comparisons** — side-by-side looks at similar options to help you choose.

## How the guide is organized

Each major topic has its own section in the navigation:

1. **Overview page** — explains what the category covers and links to specific topics within it.
2. **Individual entries** — pages dedicated to specific mods, resource packs, shaders, or setup recipes.
3. **Setups** — curated combinations that bring multiple entries together for a single goal (for example, a "Performance" setup or a "Beautiful" setup).
4. **Comparisons** — pages that place similar options side by side so you can decide which one fits your needs.

> NOTE All current content is placeholder. Real Minecraft mods, resource packs, shaders, setups, screenshots, and download links will be added by the repository owner later. The structure, navigation, and templates are ready to be populated.
